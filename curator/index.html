I'm a multidisciplinary artist based in Spain, currently studying a higher vocational degree in 3D Animation, Games and Interactive Environments. I love creating stories and giving them life, and I love taking someone else's idea and turning it into something you can see.

My philosophy revolves around never-ending curiosity and bettering yourself: there is always another technique to learn and another strange concept to chase. Right now that means animation, games and comics, and one day teaching art to other people.
