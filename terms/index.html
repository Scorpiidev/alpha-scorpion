Terms

The house rules of the cabinet, in plain words. Last updated 25 October 2026.

The artwork

Every illustration, comic page, study, design and logo on this site is by Antares, all rights reserved. Sharing a link to this site or to a piece is always welcome. Without my written permission, please don't:

  • repost or reupload my work, with or without credit;
  • trace, edit, recolour or claim it as your own;
  • print it, sell it or put it on merchandise, NFTs or anything else;
  • use it to train, fine-tune or prompt AI models, or add it to datasets or image generators.

Want to use something? Ask through the transmitter. I'm friendly.

Commissions

Every commission is agreed in writing before any work begins: price, payment, deadlines, number of revisions and how the finished piece may be used (personal and commercial use are different things). I keep the copyright and the right to show commissioned work in my portfolio unless we agree otherwise. I can decline any request.

The contact form

Messages sent through the transmitter are delivered to me by Web3Forms, with hCaptcha keeping spam out. I only use your name and email to answer you. I never sell or share them.

Your browser

I don't set cookies or run analytics or trackers. The cabinet keeps a few small things in your own browser's storage: how you arranged the desk and the oddities in your drawer. You can wipe them by clearing this site's data. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the contact page loads hCaptcha, which follow their own privacy policies.

Borrowed exhibits

The archive images on the desk are public domain or CC0. Their sources are listed in the colophon.

Changes

These terms may change over time; the date at the top tells you when they last did.
