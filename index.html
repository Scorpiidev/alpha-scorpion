About

I'm Antares, a multidisciplinary artist from Spain, currently studying a higher vocational degree in 3D Animation, Games and Interactive Environments.

I've been making indie comics on my own for about ten years, so I'm used to carrying a page from thumbnail to pencils, inks and colour. I love creating stories, and I love taking someone else's idea and turning it into something you can see. There's always another technique to learn and another strange concept to chase.

Tools
Krita, Photoshop, Toon Boom Harmony, Moho, Maya, ZBrush
