About

who draws all this

{% include heart.svg class="about__heart" %}

I'm Antares, a multidisciplinary artist based in Spain, currently studying a higher vocational degree in 3D Animation, Games and Interactive Environments. I love creating stories and giving them life, and I love taking someone else's idea and turning it into something you can see.

I've been making indie comics on my own for about ten years, which means I'm used to carrying a page from thumbnail to pencils, inks and colour. My philosophy revolves around never-ending curiosity and bettering yourself: there is always another technique to learn and another strange concept to chase.
