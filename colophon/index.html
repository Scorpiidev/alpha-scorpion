The archive imagery

The scientific and historical fragments on the desk are public domain or CC0, cropped, dithered and re-coloured for the cabinet:

  • Hubble eXtreme Deep Field (2012). NASA, ESA, G. Illingworth et al. Hubble imagery prepared for NASA, used under NASA's media guidelines.
  • a NASA astronaut, official NASA astronaut portrait. NASA, public domain.
  • a Navy officer, official portrait, 1984. U.S. Navy, public domain.
  • DSCOVR launch on a Falcon 9, 2015. SpaceX, released into the public domain.
  • Greek coins from Pompeii. Brooklyn Museum open collection, no known copyright restrictions.
  • Cell floating in saline, quantitative phase image. Paul Müller et al., CC0.
  • Motion-blurred clock. Stefan van der Walt, public domain.

The star chart uses real J2000 positions of the stars of Scorpius. The specimens in the synthesizer are grown from noise and do not exist.
